## The problem

Every month an analyst exported sales from the ERP, pasted them into a 40-tab spreadsheet and extended a
trend line by hand. It took most of a day, broke whenever a column moved, and nobody quite believed the numbers.

## What I built

<Columns>
  <Card title="Before">ERP export → Excel → copy-paste → linear trend by eye → PDF by email.</Card>
  <Card title="After">ERP → BigQuery (daily) → dbt models + tests → seasonal forecast → dashboard.</Card>
</Columns>

1. **Ingestion:** a scheduled job loads orders from the ERP API into BigQuery every night.
2. **Modelling:** dbt turns raw orders into clean monthly sales per region, with tests for duplicates and gaps.
3. **Forecasting:** a trend + seasonality model, compared against simpler baselines with a backtest.

## Try it: why the model choice matters

Switch between methods below. A plain moving average ignores seasonality, so it misses every peak.

## The result

The forecast now refreshes daily with no manual steps, and error dropped from roughly 15% to under 5%.
