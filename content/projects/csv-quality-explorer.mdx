## Why

The first hour of every data project is the same: open the file, scroll, and ask *"what's actually in here?"*
This tool answers that instantly, and nothing is uploaded to a server.

<CsvExplorer />

## How it works

- A small streaming-friendly CSV parser that handles quoted fields (`"3,10"` above is caught as *not numeric*).
- Each column is typed automatically: if 80% or more of its values parse as numbers, it's numeric.
- Missing and invalid values are counted per column, because those are what break pipelines downstream.
