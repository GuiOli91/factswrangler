## The problem

Managers juggled five reports from three systems, each with a slightly different definition of "late".

## What I built

- Agreed a single definition for each KPI with the team (the hardest part, honestly).
- SQL views in PostgreSQL that compute every KPI the same way, everywhere.
- A lightweight Next.js dashboard with filters by warehouse and week.

## The result

One source of truth, opened every morning by the whole operations team.
